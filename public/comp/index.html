Stream Competitions

Welcome

Pick a format on Home, then follow the steps: Rules, Players, Start.
  1. 1Last Man Standing: everyone buys in, and the lowest win each round is eliminated until one player remains.
  2. 2Showdown: 2 to 5 named players (streamer vs streamer, streamer vs chat, slot vs slot). The highest win takes the round, and the first to a chosen target score wins.
  3. 3Bracket Tournament: 4, 8, or 16 entrants, single elimination. Players mode adds a coin flip for who picks. Slots mode just compares wins.
  4. 4Points League: nobody is eliminated, everyone chooses one slot, and the most points after all rounds wins.
  5. 5Inside a format, click Home anytime to come back here. Each format saves its own progress, so switching back and forth never loses it.
  6. 6OBS setup (recommended): add a Browser Source pointing at this file with #display on the end and set it to a square, 450×450. That source shows only the stream box and mirrors everything you do here, animations included. Any square size works: the box scales to fill it.
  7. 7Two-PC / remote control: if this page is hosted online (with sync.php uploaded next to index.html), the control panel and the OBS source can run on different computers, even on different networks. Anyone who opens the plain URL (no #display) can run the show from anywhere. The sound button mutes sounds per window if you hear doubles.

Last Man Standing

A quick guide for running the competition on stream.

Before you start

  1. 1Rules: choose what the final 1v1 is played to, and whether extra lives are on.
  2. 2Players: type each nickname and press Enter to jump to a new row. Or let chat enter: Open keyword entries, then Draw a player as many times as you need. The winner types anything in their chat within 60s to claim (or you press Mark claimed), and they go straight into Players.
  3. 3You need at least 2 players, and every player needs a name.
  4. 4Start: check the summary and click Start competition. The roster locks and Round 1 begins. You can't add or remove players after this.

Running each round

  1. 1After each bonus opens, type what it won into that player's field. Press Enter to move to the next field. Enter on the last field jumps to the big button.
  2. 2Once every player still in has a win entered, click End round.
  3. 3Whoever has the lowest win is eliminated automatically, with an on-stream elimination takeover.
  4. 4If there's a tie for lowest, a tiebreaker kicks in. Only the tied players re-buy and enter new wins until someone is clearly lowest.
  5. 5The gold Picks row shows whose turn it is to pick the bonus. It rotates automatically after every round.
  6. 6Hit Max Win next to a player's field if their bonus lands the slot's actual max win. That player instantly wins the whole competition and skips elimination for that round.

Extra life (optional)

  1. 1Turn it On in Rules and set the shield threshold (default 10x). A shared Buy cost (total) field then appears each round.
  2. 2Enter the total amount spent on that round's bonus buy, not the base bet per spin. Any player whose win is at least the threshold times that buy cost earns a green shield, shown next to their name on stream and in the control panel.
  3. 3The next time a shielded player would be eliminated (alone, or as the sole survivor of a tiebreaker), they're saved automatically instead. The shield is spent, an "extra life used" takeover plays, and they carry straight on to the next round.
  4. 4A player can only hold one shield at a time. Hitting the threshold again while already shielded doesn't stack.
  5. 5If the round that drops the field to the final 2 leaves someone still holding a shield, a popup asks "Carry over extra life(s) into the Final?". It asks fresh every time, so you can decide per competition. Decline and the shield is cleared before the duel starts.

Final 1v1

  1. 1Once only 2 players remain, the display switches to a head-to-head duel. The first to your chosen target (set in Rules, default 3) takes the whole thing.
  2. 2Enter both wins each round and click Score round. A tied round just replays.
  3. 3Hitting the target shows the champion screen automatically, with their total winnings.
  4. 4Click Show final standings to switch the stream box to every player's total win, ordered by finish place (champion first, down to whoever went out first). Click again to flip back.
  5. 5Max Win works here too, and during a tiebreaker. Hitting it ends the competition immediately in that player's favor.
  6. 6A carried-over shield only kicks in on the round that would actually end the duel. It saves the holder that one time, the round doesn't count, and play continues.

Fixing mistakes

  1. 1Undo reverts the last thing you did: a round result, an elimination, anything.
  2. 2New wipes everything and starts a fresh competition. It asks you to confirm first.

Showdown

A quick guide for running a 2–5 player showdown on stream.

Before you start

  1. 1Rules: pick first to 3 through 10, the number of round wins it takes to close it out.
  2. 2Players: name everyone taking part. 2 players is the classic 1v1, and up to 5 fit on the board. Add player adds a slot, and Enter jumps to the next name.
  3. 3Start: click Start showdown once every name is filled in.

Running each round

  1. 1The board shows whose turn it is to pick the slot. It rotates one place every round.
  2. 2Type everyone's win, then click Score round.
  3. 3The highest win takes the round point. A tie for the lead just replays the round.
  4. 4The first player to hit your chosen target gets the winner screen automatically, with everyone's totals.
  5. 5Hit Max Win next to a player if their bonus lands the slot's actual max win. That player wins the whole showdown on the spot.

Fixing mistakes

  1. 1Undo reverts the last round result.
  2. 2New clears the score so you can set up a fresh showdown. It asks you to confirm first.

Bracket Tournament

A quick guide for running a knockout bracket on stream.

Before you start

  1. 1Rules: choose Players or Slots. Players is for people competing (streamer vs streamer, streamer vs chat) and includes the coin flip below. Slots compares two slot games directly: no picking, no coin flip, just enter both wins each round.
  2. 2Pick a bracket size (4, 8, or 16 entrants) and a match format, first to 1 or 3, kept short so a full bracket doesn't drag on. The Final is always first to 3, whatever you pick here.
  3. 3Players: name every entrant (or slot). Order doesn't matter, because the bracket is shuffled randomly when you start.
  4. 4Start: click Start bracket once every name is filled in.

Playing a match: Players mode

  1. 1Each match opens with a Flip coin button. Click it when you're ready, and a spinning coin with each entrant's name on a face lands on whoever picks the slot first. It's a one-time 50/50 per match, not every round.
  2. 2Enter both wins, then click Score round. A tie replays, and the pick passes to the other entrant. On later rounds within the same match, picking simply alternates, so no more coin flips are needed.
  3. 3The first to your chosen target wins the match. A "wins the match" takeover plays, then the next match loads with its own Flip coin button waiting.

Playing a match: Slots mode

  1. 1No coin flip. Each match goes straight to entering both slots' wins. Click Score round. A tie replays, and the first to your chosen target wins the match.

Either mode

  1. 1Click Show bracket anytime to switch the stream box to the tree: the rounds that are still being played, with every result so far. Click again to go back to the live match.
  2. 2Hit Max Win next to an entrant if their bonus lands the slot's actual max win. They win the match instantly, and the bracket continues as normal.
  3. 3Winning the Final crowns the champion automatically.

Fixing mistakes

  1. 1Undo reverts the last round result.
  2. 2New clears everything for a fresh bracket. It asks you to confirm first.

TaDa Gaming Giveaway

Ten viewers, one bonus buy each, €50 to each of the top 2 wins.
  1. 1Players: open keyword entries. Viewers type TaDa. Draw 10 players; each claim fills a row. You can also type names in.
  2. 2Start the buys, then enter each player's bonus win. The stream box ranks them live and marks the top 2 with €50.
  3. 3End giveaway once every win is in. Players tied across 2nd place split those prizes. Pay the € prizes by hand.

Slot Wheel

Chat picks the slots, the wheel picks the next one, and the picker gets points.
  1. 1Rules: set the point tiers by multiplier (win ÷ buy). The highest tier a bonus reaches is what its picker gets. Connect Twitch or Kick to send points by itself.
  2. 2Slots: open slot entries. Viewers type !slot and a slot name, one slot each; a slot already in isn't added twice.
  3. 3Draw a slot spins the wheel. Buy the bonus, enter the buy cost and the win, and press Score slot. Points send 8 seconds later, so Undo can catch a typo first.
  4. 4Entries stay open between slots. End the wheel closes them and puts every slot, best multiplier first, on stream.

Points League

A quick guide for running a no-elimination points format on stream.

Before you start

  1. 1Rules: choose a scoring mode: Total win rank, X-win tiers, or Placement + X-win bonus (explained below). Nobody gets eliminated in this format. Everyone plays every round.
  2. 2Optionally turn on Double points for the last 1 or 2 rounds so the standings stay winnable right to the end, and the Top-2 Final.
  3. 3Players: add every player's nickname. Start: click Start league. The number of rounds is set to the number of players, so each player gets exactly one turn choosing the slot.

Running each round: Total win rank

  1. 1Enter every player's win for that round, then click End round.
  2. 2Points are awarded by rank that round. With N players, 1st place scores N points, 2nd scores N-1, and so on down to 1 point for last. Tied wins share the higher points.

Running each round: X-win tiers

  1. 1Everyone buys the same slot each round, so enter one shared Buy cost (total) for the round (the actual amount spent on the buy, not the base bet per spin), then every player's cash win as usual.
  2. 2Points come from the tier each player's win ÷ bet (their "X") falls into. Edit the tier table in Rules to set your own thresholds and point values, including negative tiers for bad hits.
  3. 3Hit a player's Max Win toggle if their bonus lands the slot's actual max win. They get the flat max-win bonus (set in Rules) instead of a tier lookup for that round, and the league keeps going.

Running each round: Placement + X-win bonus

  1. 1A mix of both. Everyone still scores placement points that round (same as Total win rank), and their win ÷ bet also hits a tier from the same table as X-win tiers. That tier's points are added on top as a bonus, or subtracted as a penalty for a weak hit.
  2. 2Enter the shared Buy cost (total) each round, just like X-win tiers: the amount actually spent on the buy, not the base bet. A big multiplier can close a real points gap even from last place, and a very low one can cost the leader their lead.
  3. 3Max Win replaces just the bonus with the flat max-win points. The player's placement points for that round are unaffected.

Double points

  1. 1Set in Rules, before starting. The final 1 or 2 rounds of the league (whichever you choose) award double whatever points were earned that round, in any scoring mode.
  2. 2The stream box and control panel both flag a double round clearly (2x POINTS), and the round history log marks it too.

Top-2 Final (optional)

  1. 1Turn it On in Rules. Once every league round is played, the standings don't decide the winner outright. Instead, the top 2 scorers by points (ties broken by total cash won) advance to a decider.
  2. 2Flip the coin to decide who picks the slot for Round 1 of the Final, then enter both wins and click Score round. A tied round just replays. Picking alternates between the two finalists every round, win or tie.
  3. 3First to 3 round wins takes the whole league. Max Win ends the Final immediately in that finalist's favor.

Either mode

  1. 1The gold Picks tag rotates automatically each round, and the player picking always sits at the top of your list.
  2. 2After everyone has had a turn, whoever has the most total points wins, unless Top-2 Final is on, in which case the top 2 play it out instead. With Top-2 Final off, a tie for the lead triggers a sudden-death round for just the tied players, and the highest re-buy wins outright.
  3. 3Click Show final standings on the winner screen to see everyone ranked by points.

Fixing mistakes

  1. 1Undo reverts the last round result.
  2. 2New clears everything for a fresh league. It asks you to confirm first.
