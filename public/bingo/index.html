Board

Edit a challenge any time; the stream updates as you type. Mark done on a tile. Lines and the full board are found by themselves and get their takeover on stream.

Keyword raffle

Chat types the keyword on Twitch, Kick or YouTube. Draw a winner spins the entries on stream, and the winner has the claim time to type anything in chat. A claim is saved against the stage. Back to board keeps everyone in for the next line, minus the winner. Past winners can't enter again, under the same name on any platform.

Guess the next tile

Chat types !1 to !9 to guess which tile gets marked next. Everyone who picked it gets +1 ticket in every raffle for the rest of the board. Un-marking a misclick gives the round back. Tickets clear with New board.

Sound

Plays from this window, so it reaches stream through desktop audio. The browser unlocks it after the first click on the page.

Chat channels

Kick needs the chatroom ID, not the name: open kick.com/api/v2/channels/NAME and copy chatroom.id. YouTube finds the stream from the channel's /live; paste the live link only if it doesn't.
